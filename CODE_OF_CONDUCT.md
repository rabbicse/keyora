# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Keyora to be a project where anyone can take part without harassment,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Its full text applies; the summary below is for convenience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique the code, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise, and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and review
comments — and when someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainer privately, through the contact
details on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/rabbicse). Reports are
handled confidentially. Please do not use public issues for conduct reports.

## Enforcement

The maintainers will review every report and respond in the way they judge appropriate
to the circumstances, from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from
project spaces, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
